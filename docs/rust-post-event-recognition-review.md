## Why rejection is justified, and what to retain conceptually

[3.2 implementation](rust-event-stability-implementation.md) records 44 passing tests and **two actual acoustic truth failures**, not a passing package. Its 264 required DEV calls were execution attempts; Guitar-TECHS completes only 21/28 versus baseline 25/28. All original three full 552-second failures remain. Four additional failures are two harmony caps and two JSON envelopes. Preserve all original statuses and denominators; failures cannot be scored as empty successful output.

Useful structural ideas are compact raw support/admission/attack masks, preserving measured absence, separating physical confirmation from stream routing, and bounded harmony finalization before the final cap. Comparing all ordered alternatives and bass/null avoids silently discarding information. Minimum evidence aggregation avoids carrying an earlier stronger score over weaker evidence. These are better contracts, not demonstrated musical gains; richer honest output also explains why output pressure can increase. None should be cherry-picked into production from this rejected package without its own scoped acceptance. Root's already verified live timeline repair independently preserves supplied same-pitch boundaries and should remain.

Frozen `voice_evidence.rs::attack_at` needs a measured fundamental and three exclusive partial witnesses among the first four, each with a doubled amplitude after a stable preceding 20 ms cell. It vetoes another admitted comb within 25 cents **or 100 Hz** of the partial, then requires local peak corroboration. This is a narrow excitation detector; its frequency-resolution guard is especially restrictive for close/low pitches. The code does not establish that the guard alone causes every low-note miss. Six low MIDI45 positives fail; local 18/24 success becomes only 12/24 emitted full-path attack cases. Zero local witness hits on 120 confounders does not imply zero false events: six vibrato extra onsets persist downstream. The complete-path seam test misses nine MIDI57 attacks even with zero extra/lost note cells. Matching whole/window sets would incorrectly accept this failure.

A downstream segmenter cannot recover a voice excluded upstream. The [passive support traces](rust-note-event-validation.md) show 6,306 palm-MIC fitter omissions with locally confirmed support, but that band can belong to another voice. Reinserting them would turn an attribution uncertainty into an asserted note. Palm-MIC has roughly 9,694 detected union runs, already beyond a route-only explanation. Suppressing route changes, alternatives, true attacks or release gaps to fit caps would change meaning. The independent 552-second stable control completes with all 24 physical events; duration alone is not the defect.

Retaining 244 dim/half-dim exact frames is valuable. It does not repair C6/madd11, shared sustain, low notes or real extras. The joint complex fitter's accuracy and 2.5–4.7-second window cost already reject that delivered alternative; Basic Pitch's separate full evaluation also failed required retention and note gates. These facts reject those implementations, not all handcrafted or learned recognition.

There is a bounded handcrafted hypothesis worth stating but **not selecting as the next builder**: an onset-only measurement could compare frequency-adaptive, longer low-register projections against a continuation prediction while holding ownership and maximum future support fixed. It must separate the six frozen MIDI45 plucks from their paired confounders and recover all MIDI57 seam attacks through the full path, without changing admission or thresholds after scoring. A pass would repair an onset subproblem only. It would not explain C6/madd11 ownership or the thousands of false/omitted hypotheses. Reopening the fitter requires the earlier fixed-cell representability/search diagnostic, not another event smoother. No evidence presently warrants that larger handcrafted implementation instead of the selected guitar-trained reference trial.

Discrete bins do not supply measured cents, tuning, bass or calibrated confidence. The reference has no concert-A432–446 option: do not claim the existing tuning contract passes by echoing the option. - Reference segmentation: ten seconds, five-second step, zero padding to complete segments. Deframing removes an extra centered frame, keeps the first 75%, middle 25–75%, and final 25–100%. Bidirectional recurrence uses the entire chunk. Interior retained frames need roughly **2.5–7.5 seconds of future audio**, before compute/decoder delay. First-chunk behavior is worse. Thus unchanged reference output cannot meet <=1.5-second steady event delay or current 2.56-second/.64-warmup/.32-future protocol.

Keep raw events and original fractional times; no minimum-duration tuning to fit controls. Onset/offset neighborhood support alone is not total lookahead. Offsets are provisional until their required evidence is available.
- Wrapper traps: `forward()` is identity; exported wrapper graphs would be meaningless. Constructor stores thresholds but does not forward them to its transcriptor. No broad snapshot download (which would fetch unrelated instruments).
