## Adjudication: what is actually broken

### Missing voices are a real implementation limitation

1. **C6, S `auto/dev_chord_23_C6.raw.json`, 0.70 s.** Expected `[48,52,57,60,67]`; actual poly `[48,52,57]`, mono empty. Harmony is Am/C, 0.30–1.70 s. Missing C4 is a register error; missing G4 removes a pitch class. C6/Am7 equivalence does not excuse the absent G. The current fitter cannot establish the complete labeled observation. Whether each shared peak contains separately recoverable source evidence requires temporal inspection, not an assertion that the reference is wrong.
2. **Amadd11, E, 0.45 s.** Capo0 expects `[45,50,57,60,64]`, emits `[45,50,57,60]`; capo3 expects `[48,53,60,63,67]`, emits `[48,53,63]`. Both have zero PC-exact and compatible-primary frames out of 60; capo3 fell from 41 compatible-primary frames to zero. Their alternatives explicitly lack E/G respectively. This is missing defining-tone evidence, not merely an ambiguous root name. Capo0 A3 exists at 0.45 but ends at 0.50, so a single favorable cell also conceals sustain loss.
3. **Other E controls:** Amadd9 has 240/300 true note-frames on each take: all PCs and primary labels correct, E4 (transposed on capo3) absent. Cadd11 also has 240/300, missing the doubled C. E5 capo0 has 124/186 true note-frames and zero exact MIDI frames, despite correct PCs/primary throughout. These demonstrate why chord recognition cannot stand in for note transcription.
4. **New P paired counterexample:** the lower voice is 220 Hz with harmonics 1–4 at amplitudes `.06,.03,.02,.01`, phase `h*.37`. In the paired mixture, a 440 Hz voice with the same amplitudes and phase `h*.91` starts at 0.60 s, ramps for 15 ms, decays at `exp(-1.4*(t-.60))`, and ends at 1.50 s. Both signals last two seconds. At 0.40/0.80/1.20/1.70 s, both outputs contain only mono MIDI 57. The mixture should contain 57 and 69 at 0.80/1.20; no false 69 should appear before/after. Mixture PCM SHA256 `ed26f7e5df051a728fc7bec96c49978b50a28b5b67864af72f78eb02edb3a7df`; lower-only `283be929b23e9cce8556310d918f43c719bd6931b2c75a6b9d7a0097e990bc56`.

P proves a miss against known generation, not uniquely identifiable physical string count from arbitrary audio. A single oscillator with independently changing even harmonics can produce the same signal. Its separate attack and decay are nevertheless information this implementation discards. Neither static ambiguity nor the passing independently *detuned* tests proves this case unrecoverable under a defensible guitar-source model.

The source explains the limitation. `spectrum.rs` retains magnitude/frequency/prominence, discarding complex phase. `polyphony.rs::fit` builds each comb from the current measured amplitudes, then lets the chosen lower comb explain its shared peaks. Admission counts only prominent residual peaks not within 12 cents of an active voice's harmonics through harmonic 64. An aligned upper voice can never supply those “independent” peaks even when its onset differs. Refit only helps voices already admitted; it cannot repair this exclusion. In `lib.rs`, YIN can then collapse the fit to one note when 98% of peak power lies tightly on one harmonic series. Exact octave mixtures also have that periodicity. Temporal evidence must participate in both admission and mono corroboration.

### 1. Bounded temporal voice evidence and shared-partial fitting

Implement a short history of measured peak tracks across hops. Start with at most 0.48 s (24 hops), bounded candidate MIDI range 28–96, at most eight tracked partials per candidate and six active voices. Preserve observed fundamentals. Estimate each active voice's amplitude evolution from its less-shared partials; predict its contribution to shared peaks. Test an upper candidate against the *change left unexplained by that prediction*, not only against the current frame's leftover magnitude. A coherent independent attack/decay across its fundamental and partials can justify a joint fit even where all frequencies are harmonic to a lower voice.

Keep the complex FFT coefficients needed for tracked peaks, with hop phase advance removed using measured frequency. Phase continuity/phase residual can corroborate independent change or reveal beating. Do not compare raw phase across moving bins, treat a low-amplitude phase jump as an onset, or assume all harmonics of a pluck share one phase. Start from amplitude trajectories plus phase as corroboration; phase alone cannot resolve identical stationary harmonic sums. Fit prediction on preceding frames and require sustained predictive improvement on subsequent frames with a complexity penalty; do not fit a new arbitrary per-partial envelope to every cell. That would always “explain” extras. Permit overlap/shared energy to be jointly reassigned after admission; freezing the original greedy allocation defeats the repair.

Use the resulting independent-voice evidence in `lib.rs` before YIN's mono override. Periodicity may support a singleton but may not erase an independently supported upper onset. Persist a voice only while its own evidence continues. Do not fill missing chord tones, restore fixed octave support, or special-case C6/madd11 labels.
