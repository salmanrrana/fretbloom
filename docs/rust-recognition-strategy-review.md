## Prioritized verified findings

### P1 — Accuracy is an independent blocker; making the same computation faster cannot repair it

The frozen report records 168 complete DEV calls, 60 complete controls and a complete model-only ablation; four functional tests fail unignored (37 pass). Exact-register recall improves, but the accepted target is not aggregate improvement alone. Relative to phase/v3, settled MIDI F1 goes 79.40→82.50 and exact sets 184→364 of 2059. PC exact falls 87.13→81.06%, mono sustain 92.13→87.28%, compatible primary 58.81→52.30%, boundary F1 50→40%. Dim/half-dim retention loses four of 244 exact frames. Low-register singleton partners gain false notes. Whole-real timed deltas gain 107 and lose 176 true frames; removing 390 extras while adding 199 does not erase those losses.

Required cells remain unresolved: C6 at .70 is [48,52,57], missing 60/67; capo0 madd11 at .71/1.21 is [45,50,60], missing 57/64. Both madd11 cases remain 0/60 exact. Rock 4.21 correctly loses 83 while keeping 64/69; Jazz 3.61 still adds 45/68. Generated sustained-octave truth now passes 19 probes, but the independent late crop still misses 69 at 1.8. These are substantive gains and failures, not grounds for claiming a general repair.

The ablation removes only confirmation of projection-Uncertain bands. Synthetic and supplemental scores are identical; on real audio the integrated path adds three true and eight extra frames. This rules out attributing the overall improvement to that continuation change. It does **not** isolate every downstream routing or smoothing cause.

### P1 — The intended joint strategy was substantially implemented, but model adequacy is still unproven

`joint_fit.rs::solve` genuinely fits complex sums on shared observations, with coefficients constant over time and separate training/prediction residuals. There is no old uncontaminated-carrier prerequisite. Search retains three hypotheses, up to six voices, and estimates rate/damping/tilt/frequency/onset jointly through repeated coefficient solves. This is not merely v3 with a renamed threshold.

The remaining constraints are concrete:

- `tracks` anchors only on the latest frame's prominent peaks, keeping at most 96. `decompose` requires a measured fundamental within 45 cents, current magnitude ≥3.7% of the maximum track, and ranks/truncates to 14 candidates. An omitted candidate cannot be recovered by more accurate solving.
- The common piecewise profile is estimated once from the **mixed fundamental track**, then held fixed during optimization. Alternative rates from other partials help initialization but do not jointly infer that profile. The old carrier condition is removed, yet shared-envelope contamination has not become a fully inferred source envelope.
- `envelope` represents a fixed onset and smooth exponential continuation with linear/quadratic harmonic damping and the fixed profile. There is no explicit independently fitted release time, arbitrary restrike sequence or time-varying frequency trajectory. One frequency drives each voice's harmonic phase. Harmonic coefficients are independently complex per track but fixed across 24 hops. That is a useful restricted model, not a demonstrated description of real vibrating, inharmonic strings and independently changing overlapping notes.
- At each search depth a new set survives only if it improves prediction and penalized score relative to its parent and passes `admissible`. The latter requires ≥10% of the shared fundamental contribution and, for a shared fundamental, three supported partials among the first eight. Thus all useful joint sets must have an admissible improving prefix. A combination whose benefit appears only after adding two voices can be pruned before evaluation. This is a structural search limitation, not proof that any named failed cell took that branch.
- Six later hops are excluded from coefficient fitting and coordinate-parameter optimization. However, current-frame anchors, candidate admission and all-24-hop energy weights depend on those observations, and repeated model selection uses their residual. This is a prediction-based selection score, **not independent held-out validation** of the model or proof of unique attribution.
- `polyphony::fit` uses the joint result only below residual .25, otherwise static fitting remains. `lib.rs` then applies physical support, mono routing and segmentation. `VoiceEvidence` refits prior voices between full searches every four hops. These are additional places a correct proposal can disappear; no full production decision trace identifies which caused each C6/madd11/Jazz failure.

The dominant unresolved engineering risk is **adequacy of the model and selection procedure**, alongside prohibitive compute. Evidence does not establish whether the best attainable fit in this family would recover every missing voice. It therefore does not support a claim that numerics/search cost is the primary obstacle with an already demonstrated salvageable model. It also does not prove all analytical models impossible.

### P1 — Compute is prohibitive, but a 5×/10× promise is unsupported

Frozen native 2.56-second calls take 2.5–4.7 seconds including CLI/I/O; eight calls are not browser p95. The original 600-second resource command exceeds 110 seconds; the extended diagnostic completes at 125.10 seconds. These are sufficient reasons not to generate rejected v4 WASM for promotion. They are not measurements of portable-WASM compute.

Concrete repeated work is visible in `optimize → solve`: for each coordinate trial it rebuilds bases and Gram matrices for **all** tracks and voices, allocates coefficient vectors, solves small systems, and recomputes prediction and model-constant energy/variation totals. Two coarse rounds cost up to 20 solves per voice per proposal, followed by up to six survivors receiving three more rounds; prior refits still run five rounds. `spectrum::measure` also performs four leakage-reassignment passes over neighboring anchors. These are inspectable hot-work candidates, **not a sampled profile assigning CPU percentages**. The tiny six-column Cholesky alone is not established as the bottleneck.

A credible bounded optimization would cache track-to-voice membership, unchanged basis columns and Gram products, precompute total/variation once per decomposition, and reuse fixed scratch buffers. A coordinate change would update only tracks matched to the changed voice, retaining the exact proposal order/objective. This could reduce redundant work without intentional accuracy changes; numerical equivalence and actual speed still need measurement. Reducing the beam, update cadence or context changes inference behavior and is not a free optimization. Neither approach gives an accuracy path, so neither is the recommended next product package.

### P2 — A residual trace is necessary for salvage, not necessary to reject or evaluate a different evidence source

Do not spend another full corpus run collecting traces indiscriminately. If root chooses to reopen the fitter, require one isolated diagnostic package first: fixed C6/madd11/Rock/Jazz cells, the failed delayed octave, one failed five/six-voice control and their singleton partners. Log measured tracks, candidate exclusions, per-set training/prediction/penalty, ridge/conditioning indicators, coefficients, contribution at the fundamental, admissibility, chosen fallback, physical support, YIN and final segmentation. Include the same release and late-crop cells rather than selecting favorable times.

In that diagnostic only, force the known true candidate set and compare its best fit with singleton/alias explanations, without publishing oracle-assisted output as recognition. If the true set is representable and predicts better but search excludes it, there is evidence for a bounded proposal repair. If it fits poorly or aliases predict equally well, wider search is not an accuracy solution; measurement/model assumptions need revision. If it survives inference but is lost downstream, repair that exact contract. Any salvage authorization requires this causal separation plus a measured profile and a bounded change passing paired retention controls. No such trace or accuracy case is currently established.

## Candidate research: what is established and what is not

Primary sources were checked on 2026-10-31. Small metadata snapshots are retained under the owned target directory. Git trees identify the inspected versions; downloadable artifacts must still receive content SHA256s in the implementation package.

**Basic Pitch provides relevant learned evidence, not chord names or an acoustic oracle.** Its paper describes joint note/onset/multipitch prediction and training that includes GuitarSet. Published benchmark numbers use different metrics and do not substitute for our scores. The repository says it works best on one instrument at a time. Neither claim establishes this app's live accuracy. Sources: [paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2203.09893v2), [official repository](https://github.com/spotify/basic-pitch).

**Training overlap is a hard interpretation limit.** The official [GuitarSet pipeline](https://github.com/spotify/basic-pitch/blob/fa5997af0a8210982619003269994a1be25eddf3/basic_pitch/data/datasets/guitarset.py) uses microphone audio/JAMS and a seeded random track split. This code is not the exact published checkpoint's training manifest. Treat existing real DEV as potentially seen unless checkpoint-specific split provenance proves otherwise. An independent scorer/operator does not make seen recordings independent generalization. Do not inspect holdout to resolve this: a separate authorized data custodian can audit split identifiers later, without releasing its examples. New rights-cleared recordings across players/instruments/rooms, frozen before inference and excluded from tuning, are needed for an independent real-performance claim if exact exclusion cannot be established. Synthetic controls still test their specified behavior; they do not alone prove real guitar usefulness.

**Licensing and provenance remain a release gate.** The [Python LICENSE](https://github.com/spotify/basic-pitch/blob/fa5997af0a8210982619003269994a1be25eddf3/LICENSE) and [TS repository](https://github.com/spotify/basic-pitch-ts) declare Apache-2.0. [tract](https://github.com/sonos/tract) offers MIT/Apache licensing. Record the chosen code revision, exact weights and conversion lineage, weight-specific license coverage, notices, transitive runtime licenses and any redistribution restrictions; do not infer all artifacts/data are licensed from the repository badge. Dataset rights and weights redistribution are separate questions. No legal acceptance or new dependency is granted here.

GitHub tree metadata reports Python revision `fa5997af0a8210982619003269994a1be25eddf3`: ONNX 230,444 bytes, TFLite 204,448 bytes. TS revision `2d498f82b61c71898edf0e8dd661b99076676c8b`: graph JSON 174,537 and weight shard 742,392 bytes (916,929 combined). These are repository blob sizes, **not** total transfer/runtime footprint, measured memory, or proof conversions are equivalent. **Preprocessing and timing need an explicit adapter.** Official [constants](https://github.com/spotify/basic-pitch/blob/fa5997af0a8210982619003269994a1be25eddf3/basic_pitch/constants.py) specify mono 22,050 Hz, 43,844 samples per input, 256-sample hop, 88 note bins and 264 contour bins. Existing capture is 24 kHz. Preserve it and resample inside the provider with specified filter delay and sample-count mapping. [Python inference](https://github.com/spotify/basic-pitch/blob/fa5997af0a8210982619003269994a1be25eddf3/basic_pitch/inference.py) pads/overlaps windows, removes edge frames and trims outputs; direct frame-index multiplication is not sufficient for a port. [TS decoding](https://github.com/spotify/basic-pitch-ts/blob/2d498f82b61c71898edf0e8dd661b99076676c8b/src/toMidi.ts) includes a window-offset timing correction. Pin and verify these semantics with impulses, known onsets, irregular endings, long-duration drift and crop seams. Preserve original audio durations and scorer times.

A roughly two-second input does not by itself prove a two-second steady delay, nor does “browser-facing” prove compatibility with .64-second warmup/.32-second future reserve. Determine the reliable output region and effective receptive support experimentally. Decode completed regions incrementally; explicitly account for note-end lookahead. Do not use full-recording evidence to pretend live decisions had it. If bounded rolling inference needs more context than the existing protocol, return a measured conflict to root before changing capture or delay targets.

**Rust/WASM runtime is unproven.** tract main at inspected tree `b947b78e3e5ff01db7d276788cfee9b96b76e721` declares **Rust 1.91**, so it is not compatible with this pinned 1.89 package as-is: [manifest](https://github.com/sonos/tract/blob/b947b78e3e5ff01db7d276788cfee9b96b76e721/Cargo.toml). Unsupported padding/fusions/shape semantics must not be silently approximated. Prefer one tested graph/runtime shared across Rust native and Rust/WASM if feasible; official TFJS remains a reference and possible separately approved browser adapter, not automatic satisfaction of the Rust/WASM path.

Small weights do not bound memory: include resampled PCM, intermediate feature tensors, graph planning, allocator peaks, JS typed arrays, model/cache copies, worker transfers and any GPU buffers. ## Concrete builder package and decision checkpoints

Require exact artifact hashes, preprocessing/decoder settings, unchanged original scores for 168 DEV calls plus frozen controls, true/false-frame deltas and runtime limits. Label GuitarSet results potentially training-exposed. An aggregate F1 increase alone is insufficient: require evidence of recovering the defining/register tones and multi-voice continuation without recreating singleton/dim losses, and separately examine boundaries, mono routing and negatives.

If reference evidence itself fails these gates, stop product integration: document the failing capability and next data/model experiment, not broad threshold tuning on these same cases.

### 4. Independent post-freeze acceptance is mandatory

A separate validator runs unchanged full DEV/control scoring and real matched native/WASM/browser integration after freeze, with all current labels, crops, original 20ms centers, endings and denominators. Numeric parity cannot substitute for physical/browser peak measurements; native or Python speed cannot establish WASM performance. V3's measured 190.3ms compute and 560.7ms event-delay estimate pass their respective original limits, subject to the published measurement caveats. The historical 500ms DOM failure is invalid. There is no justification to change capture support or lower musical ambitions to repair that reporting error.

## Objections and release

A pretrained model can reproduce training biases, merge octaves, fragment notes and miss unusual voicings. It cannot uniquely recover physical source count from signal-identical constructions or choose an intended name from equivalent notes. These objections require independent controls and honest scope, not abandonment of the measured trial. Conversely, lack of a complete v4 residual trace does not oblige another costly fitter iteration before examining independent learned evidence.

This review recommends a conditional engineering path with explicit stop points, not a dependency endorsement, approved release or full-recognition claim. No owned processes remain. **Exclusive review ownership is released.**
